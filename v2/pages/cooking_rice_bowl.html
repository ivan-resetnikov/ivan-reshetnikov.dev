RICE BOWL

A bowl of hot and sticky rice with teryaki glaze and spring onion on top.

Washing rice

  1. Put all the rice (200g) into a comfortable bowl to wash in.
  2. ┏ Fill the bowl with water. Not to the edge, as you'll have to mix the rice without spilling.
  3. ┃ Stir the bowl's contents until the water turns dirty white.
  4. ┃ Carefully, drain the water without spilling the rice.
  5. ┗ Repeat until the water is clear (~4 times)

Why

x Dirty rice may not be safe to ingest.

x Unwashed rice when cooked is emitting bubbles, reaction of starch on heat.

Drying

Letting the rice dry.

  1. Drain the water one last time.
  2. Put the rice in a strainer, suspended so the remaining water drip in a bowl.
  3. You may step away for 10 minutes.

Why

The rice gets fluffier.

Boling

Bring the rice to a boil to ensure even cooking.

  1. Get a pot with a lid.
  2. Put the rice in the pot.
  3. Add the measured water (250g).
  4. Set the stove to high heat.
  5. Close the lid.
  6. Wait until the water boils - condesation starts to form inside.

Cooking

  1. Set the stove back to low heat.
  2. Let the rice cook for 35m.

Time

Japanese rice is normally cooked for 25-40 minutes, the time you choose should go off of preference.

Mixing ingridients

  1. Lift the lid, watch out for the steam.
  2. Pour 30g (2 tbsp) of rice vinegar in the rice.
  3. Add 15g (1 tbsp) of sugar.
  4. Same method, add 5g (1 tsp) of sea salt.
  5. Stir the rice until evenrything mixes evenly.

Serving

  1. Pour 15g (1 tbsp) of teryaki glaze over the rice.
  2. Slice some spring onion and sprinkle it over the plate.
  3. Pat yourself on the back.
